PROJECT OVERVIEW

Process & Reflection.

For this project, I was tasked with developing an educational video using both traditional and AI-assisted video production tools. I created an instructional video titled The Building Blocks of Language, which explores phonemes, morphemes, words, and sentences. I developed a script, selected photographs, and recorded my own narration. I also incorporated captions, transitions, visual effects, and background music to support the presentation of the information.

For the AI-assisted version, I used ChatGPT to generate illustrations corresponding to different sections of my script and Canva's AI voice tool to generate the narration. Although AI made the production process more efficient, I still needed to organize the slides, adjust their timing, and make aesthetic decisions.

I found the traditional approach useful because it gave me more direct control over the narration and presentation. This project allowed me to become more familiar with video editing and coordinating visual and auditory elements. While AI helped streamline parts of the production process, it does not necessarily replace the instructional design decisions involved in creating an educational video. The information still needs to be accurate, the visuals need to support what is being explained, and the different elements need to work together.
