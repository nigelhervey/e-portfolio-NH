01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I'm from South Florida and currently pursuing a Master of Education in Curriculum and Instruction, with a concentration in Educational Technology, at the University of Florida. My academic background is in psychology, and my professional experience has included research coordination, educational support, and working directly with learners.

This portfolio brings together projects I've developed while exploring my interests in early literacy, instructional design, and learning analytics. These projects include interactive learning activities and data-driven approaches to understanding student performance. It's also a space for experimenting, learning new technical skills, and reflecting on how my ideas develop over time.

Outside of my academic and professional interests, I enjoy running, playing the saxophone, and spending time with family.
