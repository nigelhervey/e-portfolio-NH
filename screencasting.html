PROJECT OVERVIEW

Development Process.

For these projects, I was tasked with developing two screencasts using traditional and AI-assisted approaches. The traditional screencast involved demonstrating how to complete a task using a software application, and the AI screencast focused on explaining the features, utility, and potential educational value of an AI application.

For the traditional screencast, I developed a loose script explaining how to create a decodable text using LitLab.ai. I recorded and edited the screencast using Camtasia, incorporating transitions, zooms, and fades. For the AI screencast, I used Synthesia to develop a video introducing ProjectRead.ai. I provided Synthesia with the assignment guidelines, specifying the purpose, content, and desired length of the video.

REFLECTION

Educational Value of AI.

As far as the educational value of AI, I think it's useful for getting inspiration to build off of, which may lead to new interesting ideas. AI can also assist the user when they may not know how to create a certain transition or other effect. More than anything, AI affords a user efficiency, given that the user has carefully outlined what they want the AI to produce for them. AI is not without its faults. AI can be susceptible to glitches that make the juice not worth the squeeze. Users may not be as creative over time, relying more on the capability of that specific AI tool.

One possibility with AI video is that it may make it easier to have good design. For example, you could prompt AI to match Mayer’s multimedia design principles. Additionally, some AI has included comprehension questions within instruction. A similar process within video development could enhance engagement or better learning outcomes. Or perhaps there could be a guided element towards educational video that scaffolds effective note taking, questioning, and reflection. Regardless, I think that AI for video development, when used carefully, could serve as a valuable tool to assist teachers and support student learning.
