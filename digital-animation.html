PROJECT OVERVIEW

Process & Reflection.

For this project, I was tasked with developing an animated scientific explanation using generative AI tools. I chose to create an animation of Scarborough's Reading Rope to illustrate how skilled reading happens. I used ChatGPT to develop a script, FineVoice to generate the narration, and ChatGPT to develop and refine the animation.

After several iterations, I imported the animation into Canva, where I added captions, arrows, and additional animation effects to visually communicate the relationships between the different parts of the model. To ensure scientific accuracy, I compared the elements of my animation to the original Scarborough's Reading Rope model.

This project demonstrated the importance of maintaining human control when developing educational media with AI. Although AI helped me create the animation, several iterations were necessary to achieve the desired appearance and movement of the rope. I also found it useful to combine AI-generated content with traditional editing tools to accomplish what AI could not achieve on its own.
