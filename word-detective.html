01 / INTERACTIVE LEARNING

Word Detective

Exploring foundational phonics skills through interactive word sorting.

01 / OVERVIEW

About the Project

Word Detective is an interactive phonics activity in which learners sort words according to their spelling patterns.

The activity focuses on three categories: consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC), consonant-vowel-consonant with a final silent e (CVCe), and vowel teams.

03 / DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT

Behind the Design

This project explores how interactive activities can support practice with foundational reading skills.

The activity uses drag-and-drop interactions and feedback to encourage learners to identify and distinguish spelling patterns.
